MileWage logo concepts

Five different directions. Symbols are shown on light (the web default) and on Asphalt (the phone). Small-size rows are real 16, 32 and 48 px renders.

Round 1

Rise

A route that spells W and climbs.

Pin M

A map pin with the M of MileWage as its centre: where the mile ends.

Horizon

The road ahead in perspective, on a road-coloured tile: a trapezoid that is also an arrow.

Coin Road

A coin with a road climbing across its face: earnings, with no single currency sign.

Receipt

A torn-edge slip with a route on it: the log you take to an accountant.

Round 2

Built like Coin Road: one familiar money or earnings object with the road, route or distance idea inside it. Coin Road is repeated first for comparison.

Coin Road

A coin with a road climbing across its face: earnings, with no single currency sign.

Tag Road

A price tag with a road climbing across it.

Wheel Coin

A coin struck with a steering wheel: drive and pay in one disc.

Meter Coin

A coin with a speedometer struck on it: the needle climbs, so does the wage.

Coin Pin

A map pin whose head is a coin with a road across it.

Range

A battery or fuel bar whose charge is coins: how far you can go, what it earns.

Coin Climb

A coin with a road that climbs and ends in an arrow: miles up, earnings up.

Coin Road, compared

The original and three ways to improve it. Same coin, different roads and rims.
