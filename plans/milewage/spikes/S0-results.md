No claim changes a Gate 1 or Gate 2 decision; none blocks Gate 4 outright, but the Gate 4 recommendation below should be read first.

## Surprises and deviations from the plan
- The plan expected claims 1–3 to be the weak ones. Claim 1 held up well for the IRS and CRA. The surprise is what it showed about the spec: IRS Pub. 463 does not require odometer readings, and none of the three authorities prescribes a log layout.
- `db.backup()` is the one place where the spec's wording is stronger than the library's documented behaviour (restart on writes from another connection).
- Only `WebSearch` was available, so pages were not opened individually. A short human skim of the three highest-stakes pages is recommended before Gate 4: IRS Pub. 463 recordkeeping chapter, the CRA motor vehicle expenses page, and sqlite.org `wal.html`.
- A search excerpt on the HMRC mileage page quoted per-mile rates that differ from what `07`/`08` assume for the UK. Rates were out of scope and are **unverified**; they belong in the country-pack module as data, not code, which the Modularity Rule already requires.
- Claims 10, 12, 13, 14 were skipped as instructed and stay pending.
