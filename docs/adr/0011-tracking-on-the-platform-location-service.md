# Tracking on the platform location service, with our own stillness logic, resume after reboot and an opt-in drive prompt

The Expo app recorded with a module copied from Comma (decision of 2026-11-03: "Copy and trim Comma's native tracking module; no overlay and no boot receiver"), on Google's fused location provider and Activity Recognition, which also made it a Google-phones-only app ("Phones without Google services are not supported in v1"). Starting a shift was always by hand ("not in the first roadmap: auto-start"). On 2026-11-10 the owner decided that the native app (ADR 0009) keeps MileWage's pipeline (every raw fix stored, the shared engine at shift end, the server recomputing) and adds a 5 s cadence, its own stillness logic, resume after a reboot and an opt-in "start a shift?" prompt. We decided (`apps/android/tracking/README.md`, the decisions in `apps/android/core/src/main/kotlin/app/milewage/core/tracking/`):

- **Location from the platform `LocationManager`, GNSS provider.** Recording asks the `gps` provider every 5 s with no distance filter (`CadenceConfig`), so stop detection still sees the fixes of a dwell; Android 12+ may batch them up to 15 s. Only fixes from the recording provider (and other apps' GNSS fixes through `passive`) are stored. Network positions (Wi-Fi, cell) help decide when the phone moves but are never stored or counted: indoors they wander by tens of metres. Not the fused provider: it is Google's provider on Google phones, AOSP's elsewhere, missing below Android 12, and it mixes sources.
- **Own stillness logic** (`StillnessMonitor`): after 150 s within max(40 m, the two accuracies) the GPS drops to a low-power watch (GNSS and network every 60 s, `passive`, and the significant-motion sensor, which needs no permission); a fix outside the radius, a fix at 9 km/h or the sensor brings it back to full rate. No Activity Recognition (Google Play Services and a runtime permission).
- **Resume after a kill or a reboot.** `START_STICKY`, a restart alarm, and a boot receiver (`BOOT_COMPLETED`, `MY_PACKAGE_REPLACED`) resume a running shift. A gap is recorded as an interruption for the engine; distance is never invented. Without "Allow all the time" Android 14+ refuses the restart from the background, and the driver gets "Your shift is not recording. Open MileWage to keep recording it."
- **The drive prompt is opt-in and never starts a shift by itself.** (Removed on 2026-11-11: a shift starts only from Start shift, and with no shift running the service is stopped.) Settings > "Ask to start a shift when I seem to be driving", off by default, needs "Allow all the time" and shows a silent "Watching for drives" notification. `DriveDetector` notices a car within about a minute and a bicycle or scooter within about 4 minutes, and asks "You seem to be driving." / "Start a shift? It counts from when the drive began." with Start and Not now. Start keeps the drive's fixes under the new shift; Not now quiets it for that drive and an hour.
- **"Still on shift?"** once per still period after 30 minutes (Settings: 15 to 120 minutes, or off), with Keep going and End shift. It never ends a shift by itself.

## Considered options

- **Keep Comma's module on the fused provider and Activity Recognition**: rejected with Google Play Services (ADR 0009).
- **Automatic start and end of shifts** (as MileIQ and OpenMiles do): rejected. A shift is the driver's working time and the tax record; a wrong automatic start records private driving as work. The prompt asks; the driver decides.
- **A 10 s cadence with a 20 m distance filter, as before**: rejected. The filter dropped the fixes stop detection needs while the phone is still, and 5 s follows city turns better; the stillness logic pays for it in battery.
- **No boot receiver** (the 2026-11-03 rule): reversed. A shift lost to an overnight update or a reboot mid-shift is worse than the receiver's cost, and the receiver only resumes what the driver started.

## Consequences

- Reversed or amended rows in `DECISIONS.md`: Comma's module and "no boot receiver", "Phones without Google services are not supported in v1", and "auto-start" (a prompt is in; automatic start stays out).
- The manifest asks for `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` and `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`; both are in `policyPermissions`. No `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`, no activity recognition.
- What must be proven in the field (the checklist in `TODO.md`), because none of it has run on a real phone: how fast the GPS wakes after a stop (a late wake-up costs at most about a minute of straight line), battery over a long shift, recording through a tunnel, after a kill, a force stop and a reboot, phone makers' battery killers, and all of it on a phone without Google services.
